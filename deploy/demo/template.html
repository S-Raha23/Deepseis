DeepSeis

Self-supervised seismic denoising that keeps the faults, followed by automated fault and facies interpretation. It is trained on the real F3 Netherlands survey and never needs a clean training example.

Denoising, checked against the answer

The published F3 volume is already commercially processed and carries very little noise, so on raw data a well-calibrated denoiser should change almost nothing. To show what it does, known noise is added to held-out inlines the model never trained on, and the output is scored against the original. These are all the inlines in the benchmark.

Benchmark

Every method is scored on the same held-out inlines with the same added noise, against the original section. Higher SNR and SSIM are better; energy removed is the share of the input variance taken out.
